\section{Setup}

Let $f \in \Sigma(p,M)$ be a H\"older-regular Hurst profile on $(0,1)$ in the sense
of the source paper, let $t \in (0,1)$ be a fixed interior point, and write
\[
h = f(t), \qquad \psi = 2 - 2h \in (0,\tfrac12), \qquad c = h(2h-1) > 0 .
\]
The data are the centered Gaussian grid features of the harmonizable-type model
with profile $f$; the estimator is the local-polynomial log-square statistic with
weights $w_{n,i}$ of order $r$ (degree $r-1$ design, $\sum_i w_{n,i}=1$), bandwidth
$\delta_n = n^{-\gamma}$, active scale $S_n = n\delta_n$, and the clipped
calibration
\[
\widehat G_n = \sum_{i} w_{n,i}\,\log\bigl(Y_{n,i}^{\,2}\bigr),
\qquad
\widehat H_n = T_n\!\left(\frac{c_\sigma - \widehat G_n}{2\log n}\right),
\]
where $Y_{n,i}$ are the standardized first-stride increment features (unit
known scale $\sigma=1$) and $T_n$ clips its argument to $[0,1]$;
where $T_n$ clips to $[0,1]$ and $c_\sigma = \mathbb{E}\log Z^2$ with
$Z \sim N(0,1)$ (the pinned center; in the formalization this constant is
\texttt{gaussianLogSquareMean}).

Let $\omega_r$ be the equivalent kernel of the local polynomial fit and let
$K$ be the Hilbert--Schmidt operator of the (unweighted, exponent-$\psi$,
coefficient-$c$) Riesz kernel on $L^2$. Define the compact self-adjoint operator
\[
B \;=\; K^{1/2} M_{\omega_r} K^{1/2},
\]
and let $\lambda = (\lambda_j)_{j\ge0}$ be its eigenvalue sequence retaining every
nonzero eigenvalue with multiplicity, ordered with positive and negative parts
separately non-increasing and interleaved.

\begin{condition}[Revised assumptions]\label{cond:main}\ \\
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(R1)] $p \ge 1$; $f \in \Sigma(p,M)$ with constant $M$.
\item[(R2)] $\tfrac12 < a \le f(x) \le b < 1$ for all $x \in (0,1)$.
\item[(R3)] Long memory at the estimation point: $h = f(t) > \tfrac34$.
\item[(R4)] Bandwidth compatibility (the \emph{$b$-band}):
      $(1-h)^2 < 1 - b$; equivalently, at $\gamma = h$,
      $(1-\gamma)\psi < 2-2b$.
\item[(R5)] Bandwidth pinned at the feasible point $\gamma = h$,
      i.e.\ $\delta_n = n^{-h}$ (so $S_n = n^{1-h}$).
\item[(R6)] Fixed local-polynomial order $r \ge 0$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{condition}

\noindent The band (R4) is nonempty jointly with (R2)--(R3): one needs
$h \le b < 1-(1-h)^2$, and $h < 1-(1-h)^2$ holds for every $h\in(0,1)$.

\section{Main theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Revised Theorem 3.3(iii) $+$ 3.4, long-memory branch]\label{thm:main}
Under \textup{(R1)}--\textup{(R6)}, with $Q$ the centered second Gaussian chaos
\[
Q \;=\; \sum_{j\ge0} \lambda_j\,(Z_j^2 - 1), \qquad (Z_j)_{j\ge0}
\;\overset{\text{iid}}{\sim}\; N(0,1),
\]
(the series converging in $L^2$; $\operatorname{Var} Q = 2\sum_j \lambda_j^2$),
the truth-centered estimator satisfies
\[
\boxed{\;\; 2\,S_n^{\psi}\,\log n\,\bigl(\widehat H_n - h\bigr)
\;\Longrightarrow\; -\,Q . \;\;}
\]
The sign is $-Q$: the reflection relative to the naive inversion is a
substantive part of the repair (it changes the law whenever $Q$ is asymmetric),
and it is retained here exactly as in the corrected mathematics
(\texttt{direct\_proofs}/14).
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[No tuning constants]\label{cor:zero}
Under (R1)--(R4), (R6) alone — with the near/far cut radius and every scalar
rate parameter instantiated internally at the midpoint of the admissible window
— the conclusion of Theorem~\ref{thm:main} holds. The center constant
$c_\sigma$ and the projection band $d = (1-h)/2$ are likewise internal.
\end{corollary}

\section{The honest rates behind the theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Drift decomposition; revised E5]\label{prop:rates}
Under \textup{(R1)}--\textup{(R6)} the deterministic drift splits as
\[
2 S_n^{\psi}\log n\,\bigl|\mathbb{E}\widehat H_n - h\bigr|
\;\le\;
\underbrace{C_1\,\frac{\log n}{n^{\,h - 2(1-h)^2}}}_{\text{local-smoothing term}}
\;+\;
\underbrace{C_2\, n^{-(2-2b) - 2(1-h)^2}}_{\text{increment-mismatch term}}
\;+\;
\underbrace{C_3\,\frac{1}{n^{2(1-h)^2}\log n}}_{\text{clipping term}},
\]
where the first exponent is positive since $h>3/4 > 2(1-h)^2$, the second is
positive exactly under the $b$-band \textup{(R4)}, and the third vanishes
unconditionally. Moreover the increment-mismatch rate
$n^{-(2-2b)}$ is attained exactly: no per-row expansion order reaches the rate
$n^{-h}$, and consequently the manuscript's original bias envelope
$|\mathbb{E}\widehat H_n - h| \le C n^{-h}$ fails for every non-constant
profile with $f'(t)\neq 0$ (the window refutations are machine-checked; the
lower-bound statement is derived by hand and registered).
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}
Theorem~\ref{thm:main} is therefore proved \emph{through} the honest rates: the
truth-centered limit needs only the drift to vanish, not an $n^{-h}$-rate bias
envelope, and the drift condition reduces exactly to the $b$-band (R4).
\end{remark}

\section{Deviations from the manuscript and the repair plan}\label{sec:dev}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Sign.} The limit is $-Q$, not $Q$ (substantive; matches the
      corrected mathematics of file 14, confirmed in Lean).
\item \textbf{Bandwidth system.} The manuscript's original bandwidth/all-rows
      combination is unsatisfiable; both refutations are machine-checked. The
      revised window is (R4)--(R5), exactly the file-22 grid condition
      $(1-\gamma)\psi < 2-2b$ instantiated at $\gamma=h$.
\item \textbf{Lower bound on $a$.} (R2) requires $a > \tfrac12$, absent from the
      manuscript; it is needed by the second-order mixed-power machinery. Mild
      strengthening, satisfiable.
\item \textbf{$\gamma$ pinned.} The honest-rate closure is assembled at the
      single feasible point $\gamma = h$; the manuscript's window allows other
      $\gamma$ (the general-$\gamma$ chain exists in the repo with a legacy
      bias premise). Coverage regions overlap but neither contains the other.
\item \textbf{Scale.} Known scale pinned at $\sigma = 1$
      ($c_\sigma=\mathbb{E}\log Z^2$); the $\sigma$-rescaled known-scale
      variant and the unknown-scale extension (file 24, E6--E10) are not
      formalized.
\item \textbf{Honest bias rates.} The drift uses the attained rate
      $n^{-(2-2b)}$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:rates}); the plan's presumed
      faster decay ($n^{-(2-b)}$, then $n^{-\gamma}$ envelopes) was refuted
      during formalization and is registered, not papered over.
\item \textbf{Short-memory and critical branches} of Theorem 3.3, the minimax
      program, and all two-dimensional extensions are outside this closure and
      remain as separately registered work.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Confirmed errors in the original proofs}

(1) Critical variance constant: $(9/16)\int\omega^2$, not the kernel center value
(S.3.2). (2) Long-memory sign: the inverted limit is $-Q$; a legal nonnegative
equivalent-kernel instance has third moments of opposite sign (\S1--2 here;
machine-checked). (3) Theorem 3.5 means are $-2R$ ($\widehat G$) and $2R$
($\widehat H$), not $R$. (4) The growing-lag remainder of 8.2(iv) is too strong;
$O(k^{-2}+k\log n/n+(k/n)^{\psi})$ must be retained. (5) The quadrature argument
of 8.4 is invalid; replaced by direct design-matrix reproduction (Lean-complete).
(6) 8.5 mixes $Z$ and $Z-EZ$ centerings; the dimension is $S$, not $d$. (7) S.1.1
fails as a complex Lebesgue integral near $0$. (8) S.2.1 lacks hypotheses
($h=0$; $q=2$, $H\to1$). (9) S.2.3's $q=1$ near-diagonal remainder has a
linear-$H$ counterexample. (10) The $c_h$ formulas of S.2.2/S.3.1 miss a factor
$\tfrac12$. (11) S.3.2's critical center-value derivation and the $2!$ Hermite
factor. (12) S.5.1's spatial-average bound misses an $m$ factor (power-order
counterexample at $H=7/8$; the repaired bound preserves the main rates). (13)
S.6.1's unnormalized covariance claim fails already for Brownian motion
(counterexample machine-checked at the scalar level). (14) S.7.2's $d=2,3$
optimality assertion has no corresponding lower bound in the manuscript itself.

\subsection{Refutations formalized during verification}

The verification chain itself formalized six impossibility results about the
proof infrastructure: the all-rows-nonempty premise (\texttt{hm}), the spectral
constant (\texttt{hconst}) and the unnormalized correlation-energy premise
(\texttt{hE2}) are each refuted by Lean contradiction theorems; the first- and
second-order bias windows are empty at $\gamma=f(t)$ on the long-memory band;
the second-order increment rate $n^{-(2-b)}$ is unattainable (honest rate
$n^{-(2-2b)}$, Proposition~\ref{prop:rates}); and feeding the crude fluctuation
bound into the drift yields a non-vanishing $O(1)$ term (the tail-based
transfer is the only correct route). Four lemmas that looked problematic were
positively validated, most notably the spectral row-nondegeneracy
(\texttt{normalizedVaryingIncrement\_ne\_zero}), which is stronger than the
plan anticipated.
